BJJ Check-In System

Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Jinja2 · Railway

QR-based attendance system for the UWF Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Club, a 30+ member organization. Replaced a paper sign-in sheet and is used at the door every session.

  • Members check in from a phone at the door. Admins get a dashboard instead of a spreadsheet.
  • Retention view tracks where members came from and flags anyone inactive for 21+ days.
  • Postgres upserts and unique constraints prevent duplicate records when several people check in at once.
  • Raw SQL over psycopg3, no ORM. Deployed on Railway with bcrypt-hashed admin auth and signed session cookies.
